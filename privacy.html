Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

K-Bridge ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and profile photo when you create an account.
  • Profile information: Bio, Korean language level, interests, and other details you choose to share.
  • Usage data: Events you attend, clubs you join, and interactions within the app.
  • Messages: Chat messages sent within groups and clubs. These are stored securely on our servers.
  • Device information: Push notification tokens, device type, and operating system version for notification delivery.
  • Payment information: Event ticket purchases are processed via PayPal. We store transaction records (amount, event, timestamp) but do not store your payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the K-Bridge app and services.
  • Connect you with the Mongolian community in Korea.
  • Send push notifications about chat messages, events, and community activity.
  • Process event ticket payments.
  • Display your profile to other members within the app.
  • Analyze app usage to improve features and performance.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • Firebase (Google): Our backend infrastructure for authentication, database, and storage.
  • Expo: Push notification delivery service.
  • PayPal: Payment processing for event tickets.
  • Law enforcement: When required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers. We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and Firebase Security Rules to protect your information from unauthorized access.

Chat messages and profile data are retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

5. Push Notifications

We send push notifications to inform you about new chat messages, events, and community updates. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings or within the K-Bridge app settings.

6. User Content

Photos, messages, and other content you share within K-Bridge are visible to other app members. Please do not share sensitive personal information in public groups or posts.

7. Children's Privacy

K-Bridge is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of push notifications.
  • Export your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at km.bridge.26@gmail.com.

9. Cookies and Tracking

The K-Bridge mobile app does not use cookies. Our website may use minimal analytics to understand visitor traffic. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of K-Bridge after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

  • Email: km.bridge.26@gmail.com
  • Instagram: @the_kbridge

This policy applies to all users of the K-Bridge mobile application (iOS and Android) and the K-Bridge website.
