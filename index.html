Privacy Policy

K-Bridge ("we", "our", or "us") is a community app for Mongolians living in Korea. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

By using K-Bridge, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information:

  • Full name and email address
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Location (optional, text only)
  • Bio and Instagram handle (optional)

Usage information:

  • Messages you send in group chats
  • Events you attend or create
  • Clubs you join
  • Posts and comments you publish

Device information:

  • Push notification token (to deliver notifications)
  • Device type and operating system (collected automatically by Firebase)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the K-Bridge community platform
  • To display your profile to other community members
  • To send you push notifications about new messages and events
  • To calculate your community tier and award badges
  • To process event payments via PayPal
  • To improve the app experience

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, file storage, and cloud functions. Privacy Policy
  • Expo — push notification delivery. Privacy Policy
  • PayPal — event payment processing. Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign-In / Google Sign-In — optional social login

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. Your profile information (name, photo, bio) is visible to other authenticated K-Bridge members. Messages sent in group chats are visible to all members of that group.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at km.bridge.26@gmail.com and we will process your request within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

K-Bridge is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including Firebase security rules, encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), and authentication tokens to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time via your device settings

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date below and, where appropriate, through the app. Continued use of K-Bridge after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
